Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

In short: you can cancel your subscription at any time; it ends with the paid term and you won't be charged again. DigiSkill subscriptions are not refunded, except in the cases in section 3.

This policy applies to DigiSkill subscriptions. DigiSkill is available on Enterprise plans only. DigiSkill is a trade name of Ambysto Technologies LLC, a limited liability company organized under the laws of Wyoming, United States. This policy forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. How to cancel

Cancel by emailing business@digiskill.ai from the email address on your account. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You keep access until then and will not be charged again.

2. Refunds

No refunds, including within 14 days of your first payment or a renewal. Cancel before the next renewal date to avoid the next charge; access continues until the paid term ends. The cases in section 3 still apply.

3. When we always refund

  • You were charged twice or charged an incorrect amount.
  • You were charged after cancelling correctly.
  • We could not deliver the Service you paid for, or removed a material paid feature. In that case we refund the unused part of the prepaid period.

4. Payments to community owners

Payments that members make to a community or course hosted on DigiSkill are made to that community's owner, not to us. Refund requests for those payments must go to the community owner, under their own refund terms.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. The time it takes to appear on your statement depends on your bank or card issuer.

6. Contact

For any billing question, email business@digiskill.ai with your account or purchase email and your invoice number. We reply within one business day.
